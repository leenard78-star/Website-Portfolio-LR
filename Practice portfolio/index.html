How it works

  1. You send a report

    Describe the problem and where it is. A photo helps, but isn’t required.

  2. We check and pass it on

    Volunteers review each report and send it to the barangay office or the right service.

  3. You see the result

    Every report shows a status: Received, In progress, or Resolved.
